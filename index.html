The Notifier

Rest easy. Don't miss what matters.

An Android app that turns the notifications and meetings you can't miss into an alarm that rings until you respond. It is currently in closed testing.

Why we started

The Notifier began as our founder's own fix. Working remotely across time zones meant late-night messages like "Can you hop in?", and an ordinary notification wasn't always enough to wake up for. The answer was an app that makes the important ones ring like an alarm, so the laptop could finally be closed for the night. Read the full story.

We believe technology should help us live better, not demand more of our attention.
