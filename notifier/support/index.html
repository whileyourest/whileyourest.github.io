Getting set up

Open the app and tap Finish setup. Allow each item on the list:

  1. Notification access: lets the app see notifications from the apps you choose.
  2. Notifications: lets the app show alerts and the alarm.
  3. Full-screen alarm (Android 14 and later): shows the alarm over the lock screen.
  4. Unrestricted battery: stops Android from blocking the alarm in the background.

Then press Test alarm with your phone on silent. It should ring at alarm volume.

The alarm didn't ring

Check the basics

  • The switch at the top of the start screen is on, and the alert itself isn't paused.
  • Setup shows no missing items. Android can turn notification access off after an update.
  • The app you chose actually showed a notification. If its notifications are off, there's nothing to catch.
  • The time was inside your Alert hours (Settings → Alarm).
  • The app goes quiet for 3 minutes after an alert from the same app, so a burst of messages rings once.

Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme and similar phones

Some phones stop background apps to save battery, which can stop alarms. In your phone's settings for The Notifier:

  • Allow autostart or background activity.
  • Set battery use to unrestricted or no restrictions.
  • On Samsung, make sure the app isn't in Sleeping apps or Deep sleeping apps.

Steps differ by model. dontkillmyapp.com has instructions for each brand.

"Restricted setting" when allowing notification access

If you installed the app outside Google Play, Android may grey out the switch. Go to Settings → Apps → The Notifier → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings, then try again.

Your data
