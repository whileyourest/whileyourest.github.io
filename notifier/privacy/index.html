Privacy policy

The Notifier for Android, by While You Rest. Last updated 26 November 2026.

In short

  • Everything The Notifier does happens on your phone.
  • The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere, including to us.
  • Message text is never saved to storage.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no account.

What the app reads

Notifications

With your permission (Android's "Notification access"), The Notifier reads notifications as they arrive: which app posted them, the title (often the sender's name) and the text. It uses them only to decide whether a notification matches one of your alerts, and to show you what rang.

Calendar (optional)

Only if you turn on meeting alarms, The Notifier reads the events on your phone's calendars: title, start time, organizer, and whether you declined. It uses them to ring for new meetings and before each meeting. You can choose which calendars to watch, or turn meeting alarms off.

All of this is stored privately inside the app. It is excluded from Android's cloud backup and from phone-to-phone transfer, so it never leaves this phone. You can delete single Activity entries (swipe) or all of them (Clear all) in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes all of it.

What we receive

Nothing. The app has no internet permission, so we never receive your notifications, calendar, history or settings. We have no servers that collect data from the app.

We only see what you choose to send us, for example when you email us for support. If you include diagnostics or screenshots, we use them only to answer you, and we delete them when they are no longer needed.

Google Play provides us with aggregated, anonymous statistics such as install counts and crash reports, under Google's privacy policy.

Purchases

The Notifier is free today. If an optional paid upgrade is added later, payment will be handled entirely by Google Play. We will never see your payment details. This policy will be updated before that happens.

Permissions and why they're needed

  • Notification access: to see notifications from the apps you choose.
  • Notifications: to show alerts, nudges and the alarm.
  • Full-screen alarm: to show the alarm over the lock screen. If your phone hides sensitive notification content on the lock screen, the alarm shows only the app name and "New message" until you unlock.
  • Alarms and reminders: so snoozes, nudges and meeting reminders fire on time.
  • Calendar (optional): for meeting alarms only.
  • Run at startup: so alarms still work after a restart.
  • Battery optimization (you choose): the app asks you to set it to Unrestricted in Android's settings, so alarms can start while the phone is idle. It doesn't change this setting itself.

Children

The Notifier is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes
