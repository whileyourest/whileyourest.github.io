The Notifier

Rest easy. Don't miss what matters.

The Notifier is an Android app for the notifications you can't afford to miss. Pick the apps that matter. When one of them notifies, your phone either rings a full alarm, through silent mode and Do Not Disturb, or keeps nudging you until you look.

The Notifier is in closed testing on Google Play and isn't publicly available yet.

What it does today

  • Full alarm: a full-screen alarm at alarm volume that rings until you dismiss it.
  • Keep nudging: a notification that sounds again a few times until you tap it, swipe it, or read the message in its own app.
  • Meeting alarms (optional): rings when a new meeting for today appears on your calendar, and a few minutes before each meeting.
  • Alert hours: ring at any time, or only during the hours you choose.
  • Test alarm: check that it really rings on your phone before you rely on it.

Private by design

  • The app has no internet permission. Nothing you receive leaves your phone.
  • Message text is never saved. It is kept in memory only until the app restarts.
  • No ads, no tracking, no account.

Why it exists

The Notifier started with a problem I had myself.

I'm a software engineer and I work remotely across time zones, so my nights often overlap with someone else's working day. A simple "Can you hop in?" could arrive in the middle of the night, and an ordinary notification wasn't always enough to wake me.

I kept checking my laptop in case I'd missed something important, even when nothing needed my attention.

I didn't need another productivity app. I needed peace of mind.

So I built The Notifier. It turns the notifications that matter into alarms that are hard to miss, so I can step away from the screen and actually rest. It began as my own fix, and now I want to share it with anyone who has the same problem.

Technology should help us live better, not demand more of our attention.
